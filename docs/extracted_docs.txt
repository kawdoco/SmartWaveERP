Barcode -Based ERP System  
Procurement  Module  
The Procurement Module manages the purchasing process of clothes  from suppliers. The workflow begins 
when the Procurement Officer creates a Purchase Order (PO) for required products. Items can be added to 
the PO either by scanning the product barcode or b y manually selecting them from the product list. After 
selecting products, the officer enters the purchase quantities and reviews the order details. Once verified, 
the PO is submitted for approval and then sent to the supplier. After the supplier delivers the goods, the 
Procurement Officer records the supplier bill in the system. This invoice is generated based on the validated 
Goods Receipt Note (GRN), ensuring only received items are billed.  
Functional Requirement  
• Add, edit , delete  and view suppliers  
• Create purchase order  
• Add products to PO via barcode scan or manual selection  
• Enter purchase quantities  
• Approve Purchase Orders  
• View purchase history  
 
Inventory Module  
The Inventory Module is operated mainly by the  Store Keeper and handles all stock -related 
activities. Once goods arrive from the supplier, the Store Keeper performs the Goods Receipt Note 
(GRN) by scanning each product’s barcode to validate received quantities. After successful 
validation, stock level s are automatically updated in the system. The Store Keeper then manages 
internal stock operations such as transferring items between locations, performing inventory 
adjustments for damaged or missing products, and monitoring stock availability. Inventory is 
maintained by size and brand  to support clothing shop requirements. The Store Keeper can also 
generate stock reports and review stock history to ensure accurate inventory control.  
Functional Requirement  
• Add and edit product details  
• Scan barcode to receive goods  
• Validate GRN  
• Automatic stock updates  
• Stock transfer between locations  
• Inventory adjustments  
• View stock levels  
• View stock history  
• Generate stock reports  
 
POS Module (Point of  Sale)  
The POS Module manages customer sales transactions. The workflow starts when the Cashier 
scans the barcode of clothes  selected by the customer. The system automatically fetches product 
information such as name, price, size, and brand . The cashier selects quantities and applies 
discounts if applicable. The system calculates the total amount aut omatically, and payment is 
collected via cash or card. After completing the transaction, a sales receipt is generated. The sold 
quantities are immediately deducted from inventory in real time. If customers return items, the 
cashier processes r eturns through the POS system, and stock levels are adjusted accordingly. Daily 
sales closing is performed at the end of each business day.  
Functional Requirement  
• Scan product barcode  
• Auto -fetch product details  
• Select quantities  
• Automatic total calculation  
• Apply discounts  
• Accept cash and card payments  
• Generate payment  receipt  
• Handle product returns
